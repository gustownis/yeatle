# Yeatle 1.10.0

This release adds a karaoke mode, Rock Band / Fortnite Festival style.

## What changed

- New **Karaoke** tab next to Stems and Snippets. Pick a song and sing along to the instrumental while your pitch is tracked on a scrolling note lane.
- Up to four singers at once, each on their own microphone with their own colored arrow, score and multiplier.
- Notes are generated automatically from each song's vocals: HQ stem songs use `vocals.mp3`, and every other song goes through the built-in AI splitter (sharing the game's saved stems). Held notes are scored on pitch, rapped parts on timing. The octave you sing in doesn't matter.
- Every phrase is rated from Awesome to Awful. Good phrases in a row build a multiplier up to x4. The results screen shows stars, accuracy and the best score per song and difficulty.
- Lyrics are looked up online by title and artist (lrclib.net, then lyrics.ovh). Plain lyrics without timing get lined up with the singing automatically. Pasted lyrics or a `lyrics.lrc` in the song's folder take priority. `[` and `]` nudge the timing while singing.
- Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert difficulties, music and guide vocals volume (also adjustable mid-song), mic sensitivity, and skip buttons for long intros and outros.
- A tap test measures your headphone or speaker delay, and a clap test measures your mic delay, so scoring lines up even on Bluetooth headphones.
- The desktop app now allows microphone access, only for Yeatle's own pages.

The guessing game itself is unchanged. Hand-made stems, progress, settings and admin passwords are kept.

## Updating

On desktop 1.9.1 or later, choose **Help - Check for Updates**. Windows installer and Linux AppImage users can download, then choose **Install and Restart**. Mac, portable Windows, and Linux DEB users download inside the app and finish installation manually.

- Windows: download `Yeatle-Setup-1.10.0-windows-x64.exe` for installation or `Yeatle-1.10.0-windows-x64-portable.exe` for a portable copy.
- Mac: choose `mac-arm64` for Apple Silicon or `mac-x64` for Intel, then download the DMG or ZIP.
- Linux: choose `linux-x64` for Intel/AMD or `linux-arm64` for ARM, then download the AppImage or DEB.
- Local browser version: [download the 1.10.0 ZIP](https://github.com/gustownis/yeatle/archive/refs/tags/v1.10.0.zip), keep your settings and song folders, and follow the [README](https://github.com/gustownis/yeatle#readme).

The `latest*.yml` files are updater metadata; `SHA256SUMS.txt` provides checksums. Players only need their app download.

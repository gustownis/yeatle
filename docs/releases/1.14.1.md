# Yeatle 1.14.1

Yeatle Wrapped is open now: from this year on it starts on November 16 instead of December 1.

## Changes

- **Yeatle Wrapped opens November 16.** Your 2026 Wrapped is viewable right away. A one-time message announces it, and the button in the bottom-right corner reopens it until January 7, 2027. Every year after this it opens on November 16 and closes after January 7.
- **It keeps updating until December 31.** Play between now and the end of the year keeps counting towards your 2026 Wrapped, and play on January 1-7 counts towards 2027.

## Verification

Linux x64: the automated test suite passed (`npm test`), including checks that Wrapped opens on November 16, stays through January 7 and is hidden outside those dates. The GitHub Actions workflow runs the test suite again on Windows, macOS and Linux before building each package. The packages are unsigned, and the installers were not tried on physical devices for this release.

## Updating

In the desktop app, choose **Help → Check for Updates**, or download the 1.14.1 installer for your computer from this release. Settings, songs and saved progress are preserved.

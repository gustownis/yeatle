# Yeatle 1.13.10

This release replaces 30-second Apple previews with full master and OG audio across released tracks on *Lyfë*, *2093*, *Lyfestyle*, and *Dangerous Summer*, adds Karaoke shuffle with era filtering and visual timing cues, plays full-mix audio in Snippets rounds, and speeds up catalog indexing, page startup, and server caching.

## What's new

- **Full master and OG audio for released tracks.** Verified master and OG recordings now replace 30-second Apple previews across released songs, including *2093* (*2093*, *Power Trip*, *Breathe*, *Familia*, *If We Being Rëal*, *Nothing Changë*, *Riot & Set it off*, *Morë*, *OH I DID*), *Lyfë* (*Can't stop it*, *Killin ëm*, *Holy 1*, *LOCK IT UP*), *Lyfestyle* (*GONE 4 A MIN*, *FLYTROOP*, *FOREVER AGAIN*, *FATE*), *Dangerous Summer* (*M.F.U.*), *Rackz got me*, and *Yung Kayo - YEET*, clearing their 30s preview badge.
- **Karaoke shuffle and calibration ring.** Karaoke adds a **Shuffle** button with an era checklist and a **Shuffle a song** button on the results screen. The tap and clap timing tests show a closing ring cue before each beep, and closing or restarting calibration stops any active test tones immediately.
- **Full-mix Snippets playback and accurate short clips.** Snippets rounds now play from the full audio mix when available (streaming four stems only for stem-only songs), end 0.1s–1s stem clips on time without mid-clip resync overrun, show the instrumental skip button only for demos, and highlight failed clip segments in a brighter red.
- **Local Daily dates and Alive cover variety.** The Daily page and shared results display the player's local date when the Daily started rather than the UTC date, with clean subtitle spacing. *Alivë* songs and the *Alivë* era alternate between the Apple cover and the white cover.
- **Refined catalog and era metadata.** Merged *Don Toliver - Geeked* and *GEEKED UP* as the same recording, excluded redundant unreleased *Morë [V1]* and unauthorized open-verse *Vyne - Ball Wit Us*, normalized the *Set if off* alias typo, and restricted the *ADL [V1]* era span to 2024.
- **Faster page startup and stem picker rendering.** Home, Daily, and Karaoke preload their module dependency graphs without blocking rendering or competing with the home backdrop, deduplicate normalized suggestion keys, and reuse filtered song lists and folder indexes in the HQ stems picker.

## For server hosts

- **Brotli compression for static assets and the song list.** The server prepares and caches Brotli (`br`) responses alongside gzip for scripts, styles, WebAssembly, and `/api/songs`, reducing the ONNX runtime download from 5.25 MB to 3.81 MB and the song list from 324 KB to 265 KB.
- **Cached catalog projections and faster lookups.** Public, admin, and pool song lists are cached until the catalog changes, title and answer normalization are memoized, catalog correction rules and era covers use precomputed lookup maps, `joke-photos.json` lookups are memoized by file stat stamp, and admin activate/hide/rename endpoints resolve songs in $O(1)$ time while keeping renamed IDs indexed.
- **Non-blocking stem catalog saves and resilient downloads.** `stem-catalog.cjs` resolves the stems root once per build, deduplicates queued folders, and coalesces cache writes asynchronously on a temp-file rename path; desktop stem downloads attach error listeners to temporary write streams so aborted downloads clean up cleanly.

## Updating

In the desktop app, choose **Help → Check for Updates**, or download the 1.13.10 installer for your computer from this release. Settings, songs and saved progress are preserved.

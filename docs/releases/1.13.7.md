# Yeatle 1.13.7

This release fixes multi-stem playback synchronization and seeking in Stems and Snippets, keeps buffered stem audio across clips and seeks during Snippets rounds, and cleans up remix titles and redundant tag aliases in song suggestions.

## What's new

- **Synchronized multi-stem seeking and buffering.** Seeking to a later part of a song (such as past the 2-minute mark) or starting playback on a four-stem song now pauses and waits until all four stems (`drums`, `bass`, `other`, `vocals`) have buffered the target position before playing them together, so individual parts never drop out or start late.
- **Phase-locked stem beat alignment.** Added continuous drift correction that smoothly phase-locks `bass`, `other`, and `vocals` to `drums` within milliseconds and avoids mid-seek reassignments that could interrupt buffering, keeping all stems on beat.
- **Consistent Snippets stem loading.** Server-picked Snippets rounds now use installed HQ stems when present on the server rather than falling back to 30-second streaming previews, and allow the browser to retain buffered byte ranges across clips and seeks during a round.
- **Cleaner remix titles and aliases in suggestions.** Stripped nested-parenthesis alias blocks from remix titles (`Double Tesla (YEAT REMIX)`, `Ion Know (YEAT REMIX)`, `Money N Drugs (YEAT REMIX)`) and filtered redundant tag aliases like `also YEAT REMIX` from autocomplete popups.

## Verification

Windows x64: the automated test suite passed 195 tests with one Windows symlink skip, covering multi-stem seek buffering, playback phase-locking, remix title cleanup, and Snippets round handling.

## Updating

In the desktop app, choose **Help → Check for Updates**, or download the 1.13.7 installer for your computer from this release. Settings, songs and saved progress are preserved.

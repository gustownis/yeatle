# Yeatle 1.14

This release adds Yeatle Wrapped, a yearly recap of your Yeatle year, and fixes the Daily result cover, the Stems view after a guess and which recording plays for released songs.

## New

- **Yeatle Wrapped.** Every year from December 1 to January 7, Yeatle shows a recap of your year: rounds played and accuracy, your top eras, songs you got on the first try and the one that kept beating you, your favorite mode, your Daily and Karaoke highlights, when you played most, and your Yeatle type. It opens in a window over the game, a one-time message announces it, and a button in the bottom-right corner reopens it.
- **Your Wrapped keeps updating.** In December it updates as you play until the year ends. Each year only counts that year's play, so games on January 1-7 go towards next year's Wrapped.
- **Share your Wrapped.** Copy a short six-line summary that won't flood a chat, or save a summary image. Phones that support it can share the image directly.
- **Next year compares with this one.** From 2027, Wrapped shows how your year changed: songs guessed versus last year, your top era and Yeatle type then and now, songs you solved for the first time, and songs you missed last year and finally got.

## Fixes

- **The Daily result shows the era cover** again, including for older Daily challenges.
- **After a guess in filtered or split Stems rounds,** the song info and a single full-song waveform are shown.
- **Released songs play the full song** from a verified pre-master instead of a 30-second preview where one exists, and stay listed as Released.
- **Near-identical alternate versions** of released songs no longer show up separately in Unreleased. Versions that really are different (a few seconds longer or with other featured artists) are still there.

## For server hosts

- **Curation files moved to `data/`.** The default `era-covers.json`, `hidden-songs.json`, `song-edits.json`, `song-renames.json` and `song-splits.json` now live in the `data/` folder, and the server's modules are in `server/`. A server that runs from a Git checkout reads and saves those files in `data/`; a server using `YEATLE_DATA_DIR` (like the desktop app) keeps using its own data folder.
- The setup, configuration, development and stems guides were rewritten and moved into [`docs/`](https://github.com/gustownis/yeatle/tree/main/docs).

## Verification

Linux x64: the automated test suite passed 259 tests (`npm test`), and headless Chrome checked the Wrapped announcement, corner button, slides, copied text and saved image at desktop and phone sizes against a local server. The GitHub Actions workflow runs the test suite again on Windows, macOS and Linux before building each package. The packages are unsigned, and the installers were not tried on physical devices for this release.

## Updating

In the desktop app, choose **Help → Check for Updates**, or download the 1.14 installer for your computer from this release. Settings, songs and saved progress are preserved.

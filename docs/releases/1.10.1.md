# Yeatle 1.10.1

This maintenance update simplifies karaoke settings, smooths mode switches, and fixes microphone, loading, and audio cleanup issues from 1.10.0.

## Fixes

- Bound optional lyrics lookup to eight seconds, reuse an in-flight lookup, and prevent old online responses from replacing newly saved lyrics.
- Register song loading before requesting microphone permission, reject overlapping starts, and check cancellation before starting the splitter or proceeding from asynchronous preparation.
- Stop microphone streams returned by outdated requests after a device change or singer removal. Leave microphone retry available after a failed setup.
- Disconnect playback gain nodes when a round ends and release screen locks that arrive after their round has ended.
- Reject camera permissions in Electron's checks as well as permission requests; allow only same-origin audio input.
- Yield during vocal analysis every 64 frames rather than 400, with additional cancellation checks. Audio processing and scoring parameters are unchanged.
- Show only the music-volume slider in the usual karaoke setup. Keep sensitivity, guide vocals, speaker echo reduction, and calibration in a collapsed **Mic & audio settings** section. Add **Reset timing** while retaining saved preferences until the user changes them.
- Smooth Stems/Snippets switches and navigation to/from Karaoke with a short view transition. Preserve the existing layouts and respect reduced motion.
- Keep the black panel in its own transition layer above the background, fixing its disappearance while switching Stems and Snippets.
- Animate the settings disclosure open and closed only when resource hints support it; use instant controls on modest or unknown hardware, with data saver, or with reduced motion. Capable phones can animate; mobile identity alone does not disable motion.
- Replace the opaque logo with the supplied transparent PNG, without re-encoding or altering the artwork.

## Verification

- All 59 automated tests passed on the Windows development PC.
- GitHub's Windows, macOS, and Linux checks passed; all five platform builds also passed their tests and produced desktop downloads.
- The packaged desktop app loaded both modes, accepted synthetic microphone input, and rejected camera access. UI checks covered the black panel during transitions, rapid tab changes, reduced motion, and modest-device fallbacks.
- Chrome Beta and Brave on a physical Android phone passed real microphone capture, HQ/AI karaoke playback, pause/resume, mute, seeking, repeated restarts, timed snippets, and the full original-audio reveal in Real stems.
- Human singing accuracy, tap/clap calibration, Bluetooth latency, screen-lock recovery, and long-session heat/memory behavior still need hands-on checks. See the [test checklist](../testing-1.10.1.md) and [Android audio report](../android-1.10.1-audio-test.md) for scope and limitations.

## Updating

On desktop 1.9.1 or later, choose **Help → Check for Updates**. Windows installer and Linux AppImage users can download, then choose **Install and Restart**. Mac, portable Windows, and Linux DEB users download inside the app and finish installation manually.

- Windows: choose `Yeatle-Setup-1.10.1-windows-x64.exe` or `Yeatle-1.10.1-windows-x64-portable.exe`.
- Mac: choose the `mac-arm64` DMG/ZIP for Apple Silicon or `mac-x64` for Intel.
- Linux: choose the `linux-x64` or `linux-arm64` AppImage/DEB.
- Local browser version: [download the 1.10.1 ZIP](https://github.com/gustownis/yeatle/archive/refs/tags/v1.10.1.zip) and keep your settings and song folders when updating.

The `latest*.yml` files are updater metadata; `SHA256SUMS.txt` provides checksums. Players only need their app download. Desktop builds remain unsigned, as in 1.10.0. Game progress, song files, and admin passwords are preserved.

For a hosted update, deploy the full project and restart the server, including all files in `build/assets/`. The online version is expected to update automatically from this repository, according to its operator.

# Yeatle 1.14.4

This patch release polishes the end-of-round screens across Stems, Snippets, Daily, and Karaoke, clarifies artist credits, alternate names, and era labels, uses recording-era covers on Yeat vocal features, reorders Snippets hints from broad to specific, and consolidates duplicate `Anyma & Yeat - Work` catalog entries.

## End-of-round screens and credits

- **Unified stark-black result cards.** Stems, Snippets, Daily, Karaoke, and the `Song Info` panel now share Yeatle's matte-black opaque surface, hairline dividers, consistent `#8901b9` primary buttons, right-aligned `Released`/`Unreleased` status, and cleaner `Different version` guess rows, with inline `Copied!` feedback when copying a Daily result.
- **Artist credits and alternate names.** Featured and collaborative tracks split cleanly into dedicated `Artist:` / `Artists:` lines, `(with ...)` co-artist tags move out of end-screen titles into `Artists: Yeat & ...`, and alternate titles appear under a bolded `Alternate name:` / `Alternate names:` line.
- **Unified era labels and feature covers.** Released tracks show `Recording era:` and `Release era:` when they differ and collapse to a single `Era:` line when they match, while Unreleased tracks show a clean `Era:` line without redundant year clutter. Yeat vocal features now display their recording-era cover across end screens and song views.

## Gameplay and catalog

- **Snippets hint order.** Snippets hints now unlock from broad to specific — `Year` on clip 4 (using the song's own year when available, falling back to its era's year or span), `Era` on clip 5, and `Producer` on clip 6.
- **Catalog consolidation.** `Anyma - Work (feat. Yeat)` is consolidated into `Anyma & Yeat - Work` so only one entry appears in song pools and suggestions while both names remain accepted answers with shared progress and full pre-master audio.

## Verification

The automated test suite (`npm test`) runs locally on Windows x64 and across Windows, macOS, and Linux in the **Desktop builds** workflow before building each package.

## Updating

In the desktop app, choose **Help → Check for Updates**, or download the 1.14.4 installer for your computer from this release. Settings, songs and saved progress are preserved.

# Yeatle 1.11.3

Karaoke gets an optional White people mode, and the desktop app can download just the HQ stem songs you choose from the server.

## New

- **Karaoke: White people mode.** Tick it under Options to stay silent for the n-word and its spellings, including in guest verses, without hurting your accuracy, rating, streak, multiplier or score. Other missed notes count as usual. The lane shades each skip, and the word is struck through in the lyrics.
  - Word-timed lyrics give exact skips. Lyrics timed only per line (most online lyrics) give estimated skips, marked **≈ SKIP**. Songs without lyrics skip nothing until you add times.
  - **Skip timing** lets you add, correct or replace a song's skip times, and **K** / **Mark skip** saves one while you sing.
  - The mode is off by default, is fixed for each round, is shown on the stage and results, and keeps separate best scores. Your existing best scores are unchanged.
- **Help → Optional HQ Stems → Download from server…** opens a picker in the desktop app. Search, see each song's era, size and whether it's installed, and download one song or a few. Downloads are checked against the server's checksums and install straight into your Songs folder, ready to play without a restart. Cancel and Retry work at any point, and a cancelled or failed download leaves nothing behind. A different folder with the same name is never overwritten. **Download from MEGA** is still in the same menu.

## Fixes

- Going from Daily back to Stems or Snippets now looks as smooth as going to Daily: the game appears at its final size, in the right mode, with the tabs in place.
- Daily plays on iPhones with the ringer switch on silent. Karaoke rounds with **No mic** do too.
- Soup now shows **DC2 · 2020**. Released throwaways show the era they were recorded in, not the one they came out in.
- Songs listed twice under different spellings of the same recording (such as "The Coste" and "THE COSTËS") are now one entry. Both names still work as guesses, and progress, best scores and lyrics saved under the old entry carry over.
- The Karaoke song panel no longer shows "null" for HQ-stem songs.

## For server hosts

- `"stemDownloads": true` in `config.json` offers the server's complete HQ stem folders for download (off by default). Only folders with all four stems as real files are listed, file hashes are worked out once in the background, and at most four files are sent at a time.
- `"stemsHost"` sets where the desktop app downloads stems from. It's separate from Daily's `dailyAuthority`.
- `"liveControl": false` turns off the admin panel's "Play in game" for public sites.

## Updating

Choose **Help → Check for Updates**, or download the matching app below. Windows installer and Linux AppImage users can download and choose **Install and Restart**. Mac, Windows portable and Linux DEB users finish installation manually.

The `latest*.yml` files are updater metadata; `SHA256SUMS.txt` provides checksums. Settings and saved progress are preserved.

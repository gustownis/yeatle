# Yeatle 1.10.2

This update makes karaoke easier to follow: a countdown before each song, cleaner notes, and lyrics that stay in time with the singing.

## What changed

- Every karaoke song now starts with a 3-2-1 countdown, with soft count-in beeps, after it has fully loaded. The first notes scroll in during the countdown, so nobody jumps in cold. Restarting a song counts in again; skipping ahead doesn't.
- The note lane only shows piano-roll style note bars. Rapped parts used to be large dashed boxes across the middle of the lane; they are now regular bars placed at the rough pitch of the voice, split where it clearly moves. They are still scored on timing only, since rapped vocals have no steady pitch.
- Online lyrics are lined up with the song's actual vocals before it starts. Synced lyrics timed to a different version of a song (a longer intro, a radio edit, another upload) used to show up seconds early or late, sometimes two or three lines off. They now slide to where the singing really is, single lines snap onto nearby vocal entries, and when lrclib.net has several timings for a song, the one that fits the vocals best is used. A short note shows how far they moved. `[` and `]` still nudge the timing by hand, and lyrics you paste in yourself are left as they are.

The guessing game itself is unchanged. Progress, settings, song files and admin passwords are kept.

## Updating

On desktop 1.9.1 or later, choose **Help → Check for Updates**. Windows installer and Linux AppImage users can download, then choose **Install and Restart**. Mac, portable Windows, and Linux DEB users download inside the app and finish installation manually.

- Windows: choose `Yeatle-Setup-1.10.2-windows-x64.exe` or `Yeatle-1.10.2-windows-x64-portable.exe`.
- Mac: choose the `mac-arm64` DMG/ZIP for Apple Silicon or `mac-x64` for Intel.
- Linux: choose the `linux-x64` or `linux-arm64` AppImage/DEB.
- Local browser version: [download the 1.10.2 ZIP](https://github.com/gustownis/yeatle/archive/refs/tags/v1.10.2.zip) and keep your settings and song folders when updating.

The `latest*.yml` files are updater metadata; `SHA256SUMS.txt` provides checksums. Players only need their app download. Desktop builds remain unsigned.

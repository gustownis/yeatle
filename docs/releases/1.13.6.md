# Yeatle 1.13.6

This release adds a continuous clip progress bar to Daily and Snippets, highlights different-version guesses in yellow with a free retry on the same clip or stem, shows alternate song names in autocomplete popups, and restores missing Unreleased tracker songs.

## What's new

- **Continuous clip progress bar in Daily and Snippets.** Replaced the separate time blocks with a single rounded bar cut at each clip checkpoint that fills at a steady linear speed during playback, clears each cut line as you unlock longer clips, and fills in green up to the clip you solved it on.
- **Yellow different-version indicator and free retry.** Guessing another version of the playing song in Daily, Snippets, or Stems (such as an open-verse demo vs. the finished feature, a V1/V2/Slowed/Sped Up version, or a cross-era recording with the same title) highlights the current clip or stem in yellow and lets you guess again on the same clip or stem without losing a try or stars.
- **Alternate names in song suggestions.** Autocomplete popups in Daily, Snippets, and Stems now show distinct alternate tracker names (`also ...`) beneath the song title.
- **Full Unreleased tracker catalog and merged `Lil Yachty - Still (feat. Yeat)`.** Fixed Google Sheets HTML table parsing so all Unreleased tracker chunks load into the catalog, and merged the `Trëndi` (`[V2]`) and `2 Alivë` (`[V3]`) entries of `Lil Yachty - Still (feat. Yeat)` under `Trëndi / 2 Alivë`.

## Verification

Windows x64: the automated test suite passed 194 tests with one Windows symlink skip, covering multi-table tracker parsing, alias display, merged recordings, and yellow different-version guesses across Daily and Snippets.

## Updating

In the desktop app, choose **Help → Check for Updates**, or download the 1.13.6 installer for your computer from this release. Settings, songs and saved progress are preserved.
